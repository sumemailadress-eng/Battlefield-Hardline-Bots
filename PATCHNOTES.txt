ThugBot patch notes
===================

V0.3.13 early release (2026-11-07)
----------------------------------
Vehicles are still NOT officially supported and bots stay on foot by default (Vehicles=0). Turn vehicles on in the
launcher or in Thugbot.ini to try this. Start the mod with Thugbot-Launcher.bat.

NOT PLAY-TESTED: the V0.3.13 changes have only been compiled, not run in a game. The items marked "(played)" ran on
V0.3.12 or earlier, and even those are lightly tested. Expect rough edges and read the list below.

Launcher
- GitHub button, and a BOTS row with difficulty, aim learning and its limit. Start-Thugbot.bat with no arguments now
  opens the launcher.

Gun seats (helicopter door guns, car and boat guns)
- Fixed: in V0.3.12 logs helicopter gunners were steering by a part of the helicopter shared by every seat, very
  likely the rotor, so their aim had no relation to the target. A gun now has to stay still with no input, move on
  input and come back. The search covers many more parts. NOT play-tested: it is not known yet how many seats will
  find their real gun. Seats that don't aim by an estimate of the direction, which can be well off.
- Aircraft guns are treated as holding their aim in the world, not turning with the body. NOT play-tested.
- Fire is held in long bursts inside a wider cone instead of a short on/off timer (played, but with the bug above).
- Each seat learns how far its gun can swing and skips targets it can't reach. Saved in Thugbot_learned\aim.txt.
  NOT play-tested with the fixed aim.
- A transport with bot gunners circles a flag it doesn't hold, side on (played, not reviewed in detail).

Passengers who keep their own soldier (back seats of cars and bikes)
- New aiming, NOT play-tested. A passenger in a moving car used to run the on-foot code and could look anywhere. Each
  seat now measures what follows its aim, then aims at the nearest enemy soldier or vehicle, leading both vehicles.
  If its aim can't be read the old combat code runs instead. Sign of the correction is settled by hits, so a seat may
  aim wrongly for its first 10 seconds or so of firing.

Spawning
- Bots already spawn into vehicle seats through the game's own choice, including passenger and gun seats on helicopters,
  SUVs, gun boats and the spawn truck (played). A setting to steer more of them there, SpawnInVehiclePercent, is OFF
  by default because the game refused every attempt when it was tried.

Other changes since V0.3.1 (played unless noted)
- Soldiers shoot at enemy vehicles, also during a fight when the vehicle is nearer, and at helicopters. Vehicle damage
  is logged.
- Aim learning: gun seats and soldiers trim lead, height and yaw per range as they play.
- Helicopters fly faster and lower over flags, attack helicopters make runs at ground targets, pilots bail out at low health.
- Passengers walk up to teammates' vehicles, the driver waits for them, and they stay aboard while someone drives.
- Routes outside the play area are removed. The 3D map can show the play area.
- Trunk launchers: bots press E at a car's trunk and try a locked shot. Whether a launcher is really equipped is unconfirmed.
- Read-only logging for finding a gun's aim: a person sitting in a non-driver seat starts a 60 s test ("aimprobe" in the log),
  and bots keep out of that vehicle for 75 s. "aimprobe off" in Thugbot_cmd.txt turns it off.

Known problems
- Car chases are not built. Direct "set the gun's angle" aiming is not built.
- Some seats have no readable aim. On any seat the direction may still be wrong.
- Fill the server below its limit: 2 slots free for humans. 63 bots stopped players joining a 64 slot server.

V0.3.1 hotfix (2026-11-07)
--------------------------
Reduces server load after lag was reported on V0.3. This has not been confirmed to fix the lag in play yet.

In testing, ThugBot's code took about 2 ms a frame until the game client started on the same PC, then 15 - 20 ms with the
server down to 20 - 30 ticks a second. The CPU was at 28% of its top speed and memory was nearly full, so the PC itself
may be a large part of it. Running the server and the game on one PC needs a good power mode and free memory.

- The map page (Thugbot_report) is written every 10 minutes instead of every minute.
- Helicopters and gunners do their ray checks a few times a second instead of every frame.
- Reading a vehicle's position is cheaper.
- When bot code stays over 10 ms a frame, each bot thinks every other frame until it recovers. Bots may turn and aim more
  slowly during that. The "shed 0" command turns this off.
- The log prints a "perfmin:" timing line every minute and a "save:" line when saving takes over 30 ms.

V0.3 early release (2026-11-07)
-------------------------------
Vehicles are still NOT officially supported, and bots still stay on foot by default (Vehicles=0). To try helicopters and
the new driving, set Vehicles=1 in Thugbot.ini or turn it on in the control window.

Everything below is new and has not been play-tested in a full round yet, so expect rough edges.

Helicopters (experimental)
- Bots now fly and fight in helicopters. Set HeliFly=0 in Thugbot.ini to make pilots get out instead.
- Pilots climb straight up and stay level until they are 20 m clear of the ground. They then fly to their flag and climb
  over buildings and towers in the way. Nose-down is limited to 15 degrees.
- Attack and scout helicopters go for aircraft up to 500 m away and everything else up to 300 m, and fire in bursts when
  lined up.
- Door gunners aim and fire. They aim blind when the gun's direction can't be found.
- Bots get a helicopter by spawning in it, or by walking up and pressing E (seen less reliably). A pilot still on the
  ground after 20 s gets out, and that helicopter is left alone for 15 s.
- Earlier tests showed helicopters lifting off and then nosing into the ground. This release has the fix for that, but
  it hasn't been flown yet.

Driving (experimental)
- Vehicle positions now come from the game's own transform for every vehicle.
- Drivers steer by the direction the vehicle's body is facing, instead of a mix that could jump around.
- Drivers slow for corners on every route, not only drawn ones.
- Drivers wait out a map grid rebuild instead of getting out.
- Saved vehicle routes now ship for Hollywood Heights, Offshore and mp_desert05.

Flags and routes
- Bots keep the flag they chose for 45 - 60 s instead of voting again every 4 - 6 s, and stay on a flag they are capturing.
- A route to a flag is walked to its end. It only opens up again if the bot gets stuck or after 2 minutes.
- Drivers keep their road route to the same flag instead of replacing it every 6 s.
- A bot with no route joins any learned route towards its target, using a short planned walk to reach it if needed.
- Only human players on foot can grow the map grid now, and bots keep their routes while it rebuilds.

Getting out at flags
- When a route gets no closer to a flag under 150 m away, the driver stops. The driver and bot passengers get out and walk.
- Once the flag is taken, or after 90 s, they walk back and get in again with E if the vehicle is still there.
- Bots riding with a human driver stay in the vehicle.

Also new
- Thugbot-Launcher.bat: a launcher for running up to four Hardline servers from one window.

Fixed
- A jet ski counted as an aircraft.
- A bot getting back into the same vehicle got straight out again.

Known issues
- Pitch, roll and yaw directions for helicopters have not been confirmed in flight.
- Car steering direction has not been confirmed with the new heading. The log prints "steer check" lines to show it.
- Keeping a flag for longer can bunch bots up.
- Passengers may not always get out, because the exit key is unreliable for them.
- On mp_desert05, four flags log "no way up" and bots fail to find some short climbs, so they can have trouble reaching them.
- Bots don't drive boats. Water exits on Offshore still fail often.

V0.2 early release (2026-11-05)
-------------------------------
Vehicles are NOT officially supported yet. Bots stay on foot by default (Vehicles=0). You can still turn vehicles on to
try them, but expect rough driving and cars getting stuck.

Vaulting
- New vault carry: a bot that jumps at a wall is now lifted over it to a landing spot, instead of being put back where
  it jumped by the server.
- Bots vault walls up to 1.3 m (was 1.05 m), and square up to the wall first.
- A bot that ends up short of the landing is carried again, higher each time, up to 4 times.
- Stuck bots that jump at an obstacle now get carried over it too.
- Measured with 63 bots: 136 of 141 vaults over on Hollywood Heights, 133 of 140 on Offshore. Vaults do still fail
  sometimes.

Pools, pits and water
- Bots climb out of pools and pits on the same carry, over rims up to 2.4 m (Hollywood Heights pool and garage pit).
- Swimmers look up to 45 m for an easy place to get out, preferring one on the way to their target.
- Docks and ledges up to 1.4 m above the water count as ways out.
- Ways out of the water are learned per map, and can be placed by hand in the 3D map.
- A bot standing on a pier is no longer treated as swimming.
- A bot that keeps "getting out" without moving now tries another spot.

Vehicles (experimental, not officially supported)
- Saved vehicle routes are read from Thugbot_learned\<map>.vroutes. Draw them with !rec then !vsave, or in the 3D map.
- Drivers look for ground 6 and 10 m ahead and back up rather than drive off a bridge into water.
- Road data now saves to Thugbot_learned.

Known issues
- Water exits on Offshore still fail often, and some of its docks can't be climbed.
- Drivers have been seen picking up a saved route, but none has been confirmed finishing one. No saved vehicle routes
  ship with this release.
- A driver can repeat the back-up at the same spot.
- Bots don't fly helicopters or drive boats.
